# Append-only Court Record

## Summary

The document structure mandates that once a section is published, its text cannot be overwritten. Corrections or changes must be appended as new sections or versions, preserving the history of the discussion.

## Seen In

- [[sources/rfc-the-operation-protocol-cb01fbf6|RFC: The Operation Protocol]]

## Source Claims

- The RFC document must function as an append-only court record, meaning corrections or changes must be appended rather than rewritten in place. [source:rfc-the-operation-protocol-cb01fbf6]
