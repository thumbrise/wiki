## Summary

This is a formal Request For Comments (RFC) working document defining the rules and structure for an 'operation protocol.' It emphasizes extreme technical discipline, treating itself as an append-only court record to document research, design decisions, and formal disputes. It aims to culminate in a separate, terse normative document.

## Concepts

- [[concepts/operation-protocol|Operation Protocol]]: The core subject of the RFC, proposing a foundational, five-field structure for describing a unit of interaction (operation).
- [[concepts/append-only-court-record|Append-only Court Record]]: The document structure mandates that once a section is published, its text cannot be overwritten. Corrections or changes must be appended as new sections or versions, preserving the history of the discussion.
- [[concepts/falsifiability|Falsifiability]]: Every normative claim within the RFC must be stated in a form that admits a counterexample, adhering to the Popper standard of scientific theory.
- [[concepts/rfc-2119-keywords|RFC 2119 Keywords]]: The mandated use of specific keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, etc.) to identify positions intended for the future, formal, normative document, separating them from mere discussion.
- [[concepts/convergence-evidence|Convergence Evidence]]: The claim that the five-field structure of the operation protocol was 'discovered, not invented,' supported by external verification across multiple independent scientific and technical disciplines.

## Entities

- [[entities/deepseek|DeepSeek]]: An external AI used to conduct a formal, falsification-first peer review of the operation protocol's foundational claims, assessing multiple independent disciplines.
- [[entities/operation-primitive|Operation Primitive]]: The foundational structure being defined by the protocol; it is argued to be universally adaptable, making it independent of transport, serialization, and consumer-specific concerns.
- [[entities/convergent-witnesses|Convergent Witnesses]]: The record of fourteen independent disciplines (e.g., quantum mechanics, thermodynamics, neuroscience) that independently arrived at the same five-field structure for describing interactions.

## Claims

- This RFC is a working text, not a finished standard, intended to guide the progression from research to a final normative document. [source:rfc-the-operation-protocol-cb01fbf6]
- The RFC document must function as an append-only court record, meaning corrections or changes must be appended rather than rewritten in place. [source:rfc-the-operation-protocol-cb01fbf6]
- A formal dispute (disputed points) must be recorded as an 'attack / defense / verdict' triplet, preserving the conflict as part of the evidence. [source:rfc-the-operation-protocol-cb01fbf6]
- Eight disciplines (quantum mechanics, thermodynamics, etc.) demonstrated a strong structural match to the proposed five-field structure. [source:rfc-the-operation-protocol-cb01fbf6]
